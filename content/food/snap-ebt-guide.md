## Overview
The Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP), formerly known as food stamps, provides monthly funds via an EBT card to help low-income households buy food.

## Eligibility Requirements
* Arizona resident residing in Pima County.
* Gross monthly income below federal income limits based on household size.

## How to Apply
1. **Online:** Apply through the [Health-e-Arizona Plus portal](https://www.healthearizonaplus.gov).
2. **By Phone:** Call DES Customer Service at 1-855-432-7587.
3. **In Person:** Visit a local Arizona Department of Economic Security (DES) office in Tucson.
