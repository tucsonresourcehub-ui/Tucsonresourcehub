## Emergency Contacts
If you need immediate shelter tonight, contact Pima County Coordinated Entry:
* **Hotline:** Call 2-1-1 or (520) 791-4171.
* **Intake Center:** Visit the Tucson Housing & Community Development Department during business hours.

## Available Programs
* **Family Emergency Shelters:** Priority placement for families with dependent children.
* **Individual Adult Shelters:** Direct referral beds through Gospel Rescue Mission and Salvation Army Tucson.
