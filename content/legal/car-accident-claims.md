## Immediate Steps After an Accident
1. **Ensure Safety & Call 911:** Request Tucson Police Department (TPD) or Pima County Sheriff to file an official crash report.
2. **Seek Medical Attention Immediately:** Even minor symptoms should be evaluated by a healthcare professional right away.
3. **Document Everything:** Take photos of vehicle damage, road conditions, and driver details.

## Legal Rights & Compensation
Under Arizona law, injured victims are entitled to compensation for medical expenses, lost wages, and pain and suffering if another driver was at fault.
