## Arizona Tenant Rights Overview
Under the Arizona Residential Landlord and Tenant Act (A.R.S. Title 33), tenants have clear rights regarding essential services, fair notices, and deposit returns.

* **Eviction Notice:** Landlords must provide proper written notice prior to filing court actions.
* **Security Deposit:** Landlords must return your deposit within 14 business days after move-out with an itemized deduction statement.
