## Overview
Tucson Electric Power (TEP) offers monthly electric bill discounts for qualifying low-income households through the Lifeline program.

## Eligibility
* Household income at or below 200% of the Federal Poverty Level.
* TEP residential utility account must be in your name.

## How to Enroll
1. **Apply Online:** Submit an application on the [TEP Low-Income Assistance portal](https://www.tep.com).
